## Overview

After breast augmentation, chest tightness, swelling and soreness are common. Implants usually settle into a more natural position over 2–3 months. Always follow the specific instructions from your own clinic, as recommendations vary by individual.

## Procedure day

Rest with the upper body raised and wear the support garment as instructed. Do not lift your arms above your head or lift anything heavy.

## Days 1–3

Soreness and tightness are common, especially with under-muscle placement. Take prescribed medicines and keep incisions clean and dry.

## Days 4–7

Stitches or drains are commonly removed within about 1 week. Many people return to light activities and desk work after about 1 week.

## Weeks 2–4

Heavy lifting and strenuous exercise are typically avoided for 4–6 weeks. Follow your clinic's instructions on massage or no massage for your implant.

## After 1 month

Implants are not lifetime devices. Keep long-term follow-up and imaging as your surgeon recommends, even after returning home.

## Things to avoid

- Heavy lifting and raising arms overhead until advised
- Strenuous exercise for 4–6 weeks
- Sleeping on your stomach until advised

## When to contact the clinic

Contact your clinic if you notice:
- One breast becoming much more swollen, firm or painful than the other
- Changes in implant shape or position
- Swelling, bruising or pain that is getting worse after the first few days instead of better
- Redness, warmth or discharge at a treated or incision site
- Asymmetry or results you would like checked, especially before you leave Korea

## Warning signs — seek urgent care

Seek urgent medical attention (in Korea, call 119; verify current emergency numbers) and inform your clinic if you notice:
- Heavy bleeding that does not stop with gentle pressure, or rapidly increasing swelling on one side
- Fever, chills, or a wound that becomes hot, red and discharges pus
- Difficulty breathing, chest pain, or painful swelling in one leg (especially after flights)
