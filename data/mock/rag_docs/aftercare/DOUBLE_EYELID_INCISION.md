## Overview

After incisional double eyelid surgery, swelling is significant in the first 1–2 weeks. The crease may look high and stiff at first; natural results often take 3–6 months. Always follow the specific instructions from your own clinic, as recommendations vary by individual.

## Procedure day

Apply cold compresses wrapped in cloth for short intervals. Sleep with the head raised. Keep the incision clean and dry as instructed and take prescribed medicines.

## Days 1–3

Continue cold compresses for 48–72 hours. Swelling and bruising are usually at their worst. Do not rub the eyes or remove dressings yourself.

## Days 4–7

Stitches are commonly removed around 5–7 days. Avoid makeup near the incision until your clinic says it is fine.

## Weeks 2–4

Many people feel ready for public settings after about 2 weeks. Avoid alcohol, smoking, saunas and strenuous exercise for at least 2–4 weeks.

## After 1 month

Scars typically mature over 6–12 months. Use sun protection on the scar area and ask about scar care.

## Things to avoid

- Rubbing the eyes or pressing the incision
- Makeup near the incision until advised
- Alcohol, smoking, saunas and strenuous exercise for at least 2–4 weeks

## When to contact the clinic

Contact your clinic if you notice:
- Difficulty closing the eyes that is not improving
- Thickened or raised scars
- Swelling, bruising or pain that is getting worse after the first few days instead of better
- Redness, warmth or discharge at a treated or incision site
- Asymmetry or results you would like checked, especially before you leave Korea

## Warning signs — seek urgent care

Seek urgent medical attention (in Korea, call 119; verify current emergency numbers) and inform your clinic if you notice:
- Any change in vision, severe eye pain, or sudden swelling that pushes the eye closed
- Heavy bleeding that does not stop with gentle pressure, or rapidly increasing swelling on one side
- Fever, chills, or a wound that becomes hot, red and discharges pus
- Difficulty breathing, chest pain, or painful swelling in one leg (especially after flights)
