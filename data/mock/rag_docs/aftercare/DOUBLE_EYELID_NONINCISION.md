## Overview

After non-incision double eyelid surgery, the crease often looks higher or more defined at first and settles over 1–3 months. Always follow the specific instructions from your own clinic, as recommendations vary by individual.

## Procedure day

Apply cold compresses wrapped in cloth for short intervals. Sleep with the head raised. Do not rub the eyes. Take prescribed medicines as directed.

## Days 1–3

Swelling and mild bruising are usually most noticeable for 3–7 days. Continue cold compresses for the first 48 hours. A follow-up check within a few days is common.

## Days 4–7

Avoid eye makeup and contact lenses for about 1 week. Many people feel comfortable in public after about 1 week.

## Weeks 2–4

Avoid alcohol, saunas and strenuous exercise for 1–2 weeks. Temporary asymmetry may remain while swelling settles.

## After 1 month

The crease commonly settles over 1–3 months. Crease loosening over months to years can happen; ask your clinic about revision policy.

## Things to avoid

- Eye makeup and contact lenses for about 1 week
- Rubbing or pressing the eyes
- Alcohol, saunas and strenuous exercise for 1–2 weeks

## When to contact the clinic

Contact your clinic if you notice:
- A small bump, cyst or irritation of the eye surface
- Crease asymmetry that persists after swelling settles
- Swelling, bruising or pain that is getting worse after the first few days instead of better
- Redness, warmth or discharge at a treated or incision site
- Asymmetry or results you would like checked, especially before you leave Korea

## Warning signs — seek urgent care

Seek urgent medical attention (in Korea, call 119; verify current emergency numbers) and inform your clinic if you notice:
- Any change in vision, severe eye pain, or sudden swelling that pushes the eye closed
- Signs of infection such as increasing redness, warmth, pus or fever
- Signs of a severe allergic reaction such as facial or throat swelling
