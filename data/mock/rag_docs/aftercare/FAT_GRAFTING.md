## Overview

After fat grafting, the face may look overfilled at first because of swelling. The result usually stabilises after about 3–6 months once some fat has been reabsorbed. Always follow the specific instructions from your own clinic, as recommendations vary by individual.

## Procedure day

Rest with the head raised. Do not press or massage grafted areas. Wear the compression garment on the donor area if advised.

## Days 1–3

Facial swelling and bruising are significant. The donor area may feel sore and bruised.

## Days 4–7

Bruising can last 1–2 weeks. Avoid sleeping on your face and any pressure on grafted areas.

## Weeks 2–4

Avoid alcohol, smoking, saunas and strenuous exercise for several weeks.

## After 1 month

Some reabsorption is expected. Additional sessions, if any, are usually discussed after about 3–6 months.

## Things to avoid

- Pressing or massaging grafted areas
- Sleeping face-down
- Alcohol, smoking, saunas and strenuous exercise for several weeks

## When to contact the clinic

Contact your clinic if you notice:
- Lumps or uneven areas that persist
- Pain or redness at the donor site that is getting worse
- Swelling, bruising or pain that is getting worse after the first few days instead of better
- Redness, warmth or discharge at a treated or incision site
- Asymmetry or results you would like checked, especially before you leave Korea

## Warning signs — seek urgent care

Seek urgent medical attention (in Korea, call 119; verify current emergency numbers) and inform your clinic if you notice:
- Heavy bleeding that does not stop with gentle pressure, or rapidly increasing swelling on one side
- Fever, chills, or a wound that becomes hot, red and discharges pus
- Difficulty breathing, chest pain, or painful swelling in one leg (especially after flights)
