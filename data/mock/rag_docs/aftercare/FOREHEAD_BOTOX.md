## Overview

Forehead Botox usually has little downtime. Results appear gradually, commonly starting within 3–5 days and reaching full effect around 2 weeks. Always follow the specific instructions from your own clinic, as recommendations vary by individual.

## Procedure day

Stay upright for about 4 hours and avoid rubbing the forehead. Small bumps at injection points usually settle within 15–30 minutes. Avoid saunas, strenuous exercise and alcohol for about 24 hours.

## Days 1–3

Normal activities are usually fine. A mild headache in the first day or two is common. Avoid facial massage and pressure on the forehead.

## Days 4–7

The forehead may feel heavy or tight while you adjust. Expressions soften gradually.

## Weeks 2–4

Full effect is typically seen around 2 weeks. A follow-up around this time is common to review brow shape and symmetry.

## After 1 month

The effect commonly lasts about 3–4 months. Ask your clinic before booking repeat treatment.

## Things to avoid

- Rubbing or massaging the forehead for the first day
- Lying down for about 4 hours after treatment
- Saunas, hot baths, strenuous exercise and alcohol for about 24 hours

## When to contact the clinic

Contact your clinic if you notice:
- Brow asymmetry or a strong arched look after 2 weeks
- Brow or eyelid drooping
- Swelling, bruising or pain that is getting worse after the first few days instead of better
- Redness, warmth or discharge at a treated or incision site
- Asymmetry or results you would like checked, especially before you leave Korea

## Warning signs — seek urgent care

Seek urgent medical attention (in Korea, call 119; verify current emergency numbers) and inform your clinic if you notice:
- Difficulty swallowing, speaking or breathing
- Marked weakness spreading beyond the treated area, or double vision
- Signs of a severe allergic reaction such as facial or throat swelling
