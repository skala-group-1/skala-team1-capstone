## Overview

Jaw Botox usually has little downtime. The slimming effect is gradual: the product starts to work within days, while visible change in jaw width commonly appears over 2–6 weeks. Always follow the specific instructions from your own clinic, as recommendations vary by individual.

## Procedure day

Stay upright for about 4 hours. Do not massage, press or rub the jaw. Small red bumps or pinpoint bruises at injection points usually fade within hours. Avoid saunas, intense exercise and alcohol for the rest of the day.

## Days 1–3

Normal activities are usually fine. Avoid lying face-down and pressing on the jaw for the first day or two. Mild tenderness may be felt when chewing.

## Days 4–7

Chewing tough or chewy foods may feel tiring for a few weeks as the muscle relaxes. Choosing softer foods for a while may help.

## Weeks 2–4

Slimming typically becomes noticeable around 2–6 weeks. Many clinics offer a check-up at about 2 weeks to review symmetry; adjustments are decided by the doctor.

## After 1 month

The effect commonly lasts about 3–6 months. Ask your clinic about suitable intervals before repeat treatment.

## Things to avoid

- Massaging or pressing the jaw for the first day
- Saunas, hot baths and intense exercise for about 24 hours
- Alcohol for about 24 hours
- Facial treatments with pressure or heat on the jaw for about 2 weeks

## When to contact the clinic

Contact your clinic if you notice:
- Uneven results after 2–4 weeks
- A change in smile shape or difficulty chewing that is getting worse
- Swelling, bruising or pain that is getting worse after the first few days instead of better
- Redness, warmth or discharge at a treated or incision site
- Asymmetry or results you would like checked, especially before you leave Korea

## Warning signs — seek urgent care

Seek urgent medical attention (in Korea, call 119; verify current emergency numbers) and inform your clinic if you notice:
- Difficulty swallowing, speaking or breathing
- Marked weakness spreading beyond the treated area, or double vision
- Signs of a severe allergic reaction such as facial or throat swelling
