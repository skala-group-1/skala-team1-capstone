## Overview

After liposuction, soreness, swelling, bruising and fluid drainage are common in the first days. The final contour is usually seen after swelling settles over 3–6 months. Always follow the specific instructions from your own clinic, as recommendations vary by individual.

## Procedure day

Wear the compression garment as instructed. Some fluid draining from the small incisions is common; use the pads provided. Take short gentle walks if your clinic advises.

## Days 1–3

Gentle walking is commonly encouraged to support circulation. Drink fluids as advised.

## Days 4–7

Many people return to desk work after about 3–7 days for small areas, or 1–2 weeks for larger areas.

## Weeks 2–4

Compression garments are often worn for 4–8 weeks. Avoid strenuous exercise for several weeks.

## After 1 month

Swelling may take 3–6 months to settle. Some clinics recommend massage or lymphatic care; follow your clinic's advice.

## Things to avoid

- Removing the compression garment for long periods unless advised
- Strenuous exercise for several weeks
- Long flights soon after surgery without discussing it with your clinic

## When to contact the clinic

Contact your clinic if you notice:
- Lumps, firmness or fluid collection (a soft swelling that moves)
- Skin changes over the treated area
- Swelling, bruising or pain that is getting worse after the first few days instead of better
- Redness, warmth or discharge at a treated or incision site
- Asymmetry or results you would like checked, especially before you leave Korea

## Warning signs — seek urgent care

Seek urgent medical attention (in Korea, call 119; verify current emergency numbers) and inform your clinic if you notice:
- Heavy bleeding that does not stop with gentle pressure, or rapidly increasing swelling on one side
- Fever, chills, or a wound that becomes hot, red and discharges pus
- Difficulty breathing, chest pain, or painful swelling in one leg (especially after flights)
