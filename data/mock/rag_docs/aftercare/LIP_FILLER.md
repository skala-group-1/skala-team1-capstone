## Overview

Lips often look larger and uneven in the first days because of swelling. The settled shape is usually seen after about 1–2 weeks. Always follow the specific instructions from your own clinic, as recommendations vary by individual.

## Procedure day

Apply a cold compress wrapped in cloth for short intervals. Avoid lipstick for about a day, very hot drinks and very spicy food. Do not press or massage the lips unless instructed.

## Days 1–3

Swelling usually peaks within 24–48 hours. Sleeping with the head slightly raised may help. Avoid kissing, straws with strong suction and pressure on the lips if your clinic advises.

## Days 4–7

Swelling mostly settles within 3–7 days, though bruising may last up to 1–2 weeks. Small lumps often soften as swelling goes down.

## Weeks 2–4

A check-up around 2 weeks is common to review shape and symmetry. Any top-up or dissolving is decided by the doctor.

## After 1 month

Results commonly last several months, varying by product and individual. Hyaluronic acid filler can usually be dissolved if needed.

## Things to avoid

- Saunas, intense exercise and alcohol for a few days
- Dental treatments for about 2 weeks if possible, and tell your dentist about the filler
- Massaging the lips unless instructed

## When to contact the clinic

Contact your clinic if you notice:
- Lumps that persist after 2 weeks
- A cold-sore flare-up near the treated area
- Swelling, bruising or pain that is getting worse after the first few days instead of better
- Redness, warmth or discharge at a treated or incision site
- Asymmetry or results you would like checked, especially before you leave Korea

## Warning signs — seek urgent care

Seek urgent medical attention (in Korea, call 119; verify current emergency numbers) and inform your clinic if you notice:
- Severe or increasing pain, or skin turning pale, grey, blue or mottled near the treated area
- Any change in vision, such as blurring, loss of vision or double vision
- Signs of a severe allergic reaction such as facial or throat swelling or difficulty breathing
