## Overview

Nose filler usually has short downtime. Because the nose is a higher-risk area for filler blocking a blood vessel, knowing the warning signs is especially important. Always follow the specific instructions from your own clinic, as recommendations vary by individual.

## Procedure day

Do not press, massage or touch the nose. Avoid wearing glasses that rest on the bridge. A cold compress may help with mild swelling, but avoid pressure.

## Days 1–3

Mild swelling, redness or tenderness usually settles within a few days. Avoid sleeping face-down.

## Days 4–7

Keep avoiding pressure on the nose, including heavy glasses, for about 1–2 weeks. Avoid saunas, intense exercise and alcohol for a few days.

## Weeks 2–4

A check-up around 2 weeks is common to review shape. Do not try to reshape the filler yourself.

## After 1 month

Results commonly last several months to a year depending on the product. Hyaluronic acid filler can usually be dissolved if needed.

## Things to avoid

- Glasses or sunglasses resting on the bridge for about 1–2 weeks
- Pressing or massaging the nose
- Saunas, intense exercise and alcohol for a few days

## When to contact the clinic

Contact your clinic if you notice:
- Widening of the bridge or asymmetry
- Lumps or redness appearing days or weeks later
- Swelling, bruising or pain that is getting worse after the first few days instead of better
- Redness, warmth or discharge at a treated or incision site
- Asymmetry or results you would like checked, especially before you leave Korea

## Warning signs — seek urgent care

Seek urgent medical attention (in Korea, call 119; verify current emergency numbers) and inform your clinic if you notice:
- Severe or increasing pain, or skin turning pale, grey, blue or mottled near the treated area
- Any change in vision, such as blurring, loss of vision or double vision
- Signs of a severe allergic reaction such as facial or throat swelling or difficulty breathing
