## Overview

Pico toning usually has minimal downtime but is commonly done as a course of sessions. Strict sun protection during the course is very important. Always follow the specific instructions from your own clinic, as recommendations vary by individual.

## Procedure day

Mild redness may last a few hours. Makeup is often allowed the same or next day. Cleanse gently and moisturise.

## Days 1–3

Skin may feel dry. Avoid harsh exfoliants, scrubs and retinoids for a few days. Use sunscreen every day.

## Days 4–7

Tiny darkened spots or small scabs may appear and fall off on their own; do not pick them.

## Weeks 2–4

Clinics commonly schedule the next session at about 1–4 week intervals. Keep protecting your skin from the sun.

## After 1 month

Sun exposure may worsen pigmentation, so continue sunscreen, hats and shade throughout and after the course.

## Things to avoid

- Strong sun exposure without sunscreen
- Exfoliants, scrubs and retinoids for a few days
- Picking scabs or spots

## When to contact the clinic

Contact your clinic if you notice:
- Spots that are getting darker or new light patches
- Redness that lasts more than a few days
- Swelling, bruising or pain that is getting worse after the first few days instead of better
- Redness, warmth or discharge at a treated or incision site
- Asymmetry or results you would like checked, especially before you leave Korea

## Warning signs — seek urgent care

Seek urgent medical attention (in Korea, call 119; verify current emergency numbers) and inform your clinic if you notice:
- Blisters, open wounds or burns that are spreading or very painful
- Signs of infection such as increasing redness, warmth, pus or fever
- Signs of a severe allergic reaction such as facial or throat swelling
