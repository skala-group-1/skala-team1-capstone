## Overview

After ptosis correction, the eyes may look stiff or uneven at first. Final results often take 3–6 months. Always follow the specific instructions from your own clinic, as recommendations vary by individual.

## Procedure day

Apply cold compresses wrapped in cloth for short intervals and sleep with the head raised. Use prescribed eye drops or ointment as directed.

## Days 1–3

Swelling and bruising usually peak in the first few days. Temporary difficulty closing the eyes fully and dryness are common; keep using lubricating drops as prescribed.

## Days 4–7

Stitches are commonly removed around 5–7 days. Avoid eye makeup and contact lenses until advised.

## Weeks 2–4

Swelling settles substantially over 1–2 weeks. Avoid alcohol, smoking and strenuous exercise for several weeks.

## After 1 month

Eyelid height and symmetry continue to settle for 3–6 months. Revision, if needed, is usually discussed after swelling has settled.

## Things to avoid

- Rubbing the eyes
- Eye makeup and contact lenses until advised
- Alcohol, smoking and strenuous exercise for several weeks

## When to contact the clinic

Contact your clinic if you notice:
- Dryness or incomplete eye closure that is not improving
- Obvious asymmetry after swelling settles
- Swelling, bruising or pain that is getting worse after the first few days instead of better
- Redness, warmth or discharge at a treated or incision site
- Asymmetry or results you would like checked, especially before you leave Korea

## Warning signs — seek urgent care

Seek urgent medical attention (in Korea, call 119; verify current emergency numbers) and inform your clinic if you notice:
- Any change in vision, severe eye pain, or sudden swelling that pushes the eye closed
- Heavy bleeding that does not stop with gentle pressure, or rapidly increasing swelling on one side
- Fever, chills, or a wound that becomes hot, red and discharges pus
- Difficulty breathing, chest pain, or painful swelling in one leg (especially after flights)
