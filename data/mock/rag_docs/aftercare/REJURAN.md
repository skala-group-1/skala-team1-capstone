## Overview

Raised bumps at each injection point are expected after Rejuran. Improvement in skin texture is usually gradual over several weeks and a course of sessions. Always follow the specific instructions from your own clinic, as recommendations vary by individual.

## Procedure day

Bumps, redness and mild swelling are normal. Avoid makeup for about 24 hours and keep the skin clean. Do not rub the treated area.

## Days 1–3

Bumps usually flatten within about 1–3 days. Use gentle cleansing and moisturiser. Avoid saunas, intense exercise and alcohol.

## Days 4–7

Any bruising usually fades within a week. Avoid retinoids, exfoliants and other active skincare for a few days or as advised.

## Weeks 2–4

Skin quality changes are usually gradual. Clinics commonly plan the next session at about 2–4 week intervals.

## After 1 month

Use daily sun protection. Results vary by individual and number of sessions.

## Things to avoid

- Makeup for about 24 hours
- Retinoids, exfoliants and peeling products for a few days
- Saunas, hot baths and strong sun exposure

## When to contact the clinic

Contact your clinic if you notice:
- Bumps or lumps that last more than 1 week
- Redness or swelling that is getting worse
- Swelling, bruising or pain that is getting worse after the first few days instead of better
- Redness, warmth or discharge at a treated or incision site
- Asymmetry or results you would like checked, especially before you leave Korea

## Warning signs — seek urgent care

Seek urgent medical attention (in Korea, call 119; verify current emergency numbers) and inform your clinic if you notice:
- Signs of a severe allergic reaction such as facial or throat swelling or difficulty breathing
- Signs of infection such as increasing redness, warmth, pus or fever
