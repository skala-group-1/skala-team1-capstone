## Overview

After rhinoplasty, swelling and bruising around the nose and eyes are common for 1–2 weeks. Most swelling settles over 3–6 months, and the tip may take up to a year. Always follow the specific instructions from your own clinic, as recommendations vary by individual.

## Procedure day

Rest with the head raised. Do not touch or press the splint. Breathe through the mouth if the nose feels blocked and take prescribed medicines as directed.

## Days 1–3

Swelling and congestion are common. Do not blow the nose; sneeze with the mouth open. Use cold compresses around the eyes if advised, not on the nose.

## Days 4–7

The splint and stitches are commonly removed around 5–7 days. Keep the nose clean as instructed.

## Weeks 2–4

Many people feel ready for public settings after about 2 weeks. Avoid glasses resting on the bridge for several weeks, and alcohol, smoking and strenuous exercise for at least 3–4 weeks.

## After 1 month

The shape continues to refine for months. Protect the nose from bumps and sun, and keep follow-up appointments, even remotely after returning home.

## Things to avoid

- Blowing the nose
- Glasses resting on the bridge for several weeks
- Alcohol, smoking and strenuous exercise for at least 3–4 weeks
- Contact sports and pressure on the nose

## When to contact the clinic

Contact your clinic if you notice:
- Nasal blockage that is getting worse
- Redness or thinning of the skin over the nose, or signs of implant movement
- Swelling, bruising or pain that is getting worse after the first few days instead of better
- Redness, warmth or discharge at a treated or incision site
- Asymmetry or results you would like checked, especially before you leave Korea

## Warning signs — seek urgent care

Seek urgent medical attention (in Korea, call 119; verify current emergency numbers) and inform your clinic if you notice:
- Heavy bleeding that does not stop with gentle pressure, or rapidly increasing swelling on one side
- Fever, chills, or a wound that becomes hot, red and discharges pus
- Difficulty breathing, chest pain, or painful swelling in one leg (especially after flights)
