## Overview

Shurink usually has minimal downtime. Any tightening effect is usually gradual over several weeks. Always follow the specific instructions from your own clinic, as recommendations vary by individual.

## Procedure day

Mild redness, swelling and a feeling of tightness are common. Most people return to normal activities the same day. Use gentle skincare and sunscreen.

## Days 1–3

Tenderness, especially over bony areas, usually settles within a day to a few days. Keep the skin hydrated.

## Days 4–7

Avoid aggressive massage over treated areas. Any welts along treatment lines usually fade.

## Weeks 2–4

Continue sun protection. Changes are gradual and vary by individual.

## After 1 month

Ask your clinic about repeat intervals, especially if your face is thin.

## Things to avoid

- Strong sun exposure without sunscreen
- Aggressive facial massage for about 2 weeks
- Saunas and hot baths for a day or two

## When to contact the clinic

Contact your clinic if you notice:
- Numbness that lasts more than a few weeks
- Hollowing or welts that do not fade
- Swelling, bruising or pain that is getting worse after the first few days instead of better
- Redness, warmth or discharge at a treated or incision site
- Asymmetry or results you would like checked, especially before you leave Korea

## Warning signs — seek urgent care

Seek urgent medical attention (in Korea, call 119; verify current emergency numbers) and inform your clinic if you notice:
- Blisters, open wounds or burns that are spreading or very painful
- Signs of infection such as increasing redness, warmth, pus or fever
- Signs of a severe allergic reaction such as facial or throat swelling
