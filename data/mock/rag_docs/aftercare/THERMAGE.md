## Overview

Thermage usually has minimal downtime. Changes in skin firmness, when they appear, are usually gradual over a few months. Always follow the specific instructions from your own clinic, as recommendations vary by individual.

## Procedure day

Mild redness and warmth typically fade within hours. Makeup is often allowed the same or next day. Keep skincare gentle.

## Days 1–3

Slight swelling may last a day or two. Moisturise well and use sunscreen.

## Days 4–7

Avoid strong heat such as saunas if the skin still feels sensitive. Continue gentle skincare.

## Weeks 2–4

Continue sun protection. Ask your clinic before combining other laser or peel treatments.

## After 1 month

Results develop gradually and vary by individual. Ask your clinic about suitable repeat intervals.

## Things to avoid

- Strong sun exposure without sunscreen
- Saunas and very hot baths for a day or two
- Harsh exfoliants while the skin is sensitive

## When to contact the clinic

Contact your clinic if you notice:
- Blisters or crusting
- Numbness or surface irregularities that do not settle
- Swelling, bruising or pain that is getting worse after the first few days instead of better
- Redness, warmth or discharge at a treated or incision site
- Asymmetry or results you would like checked, especially before you leave Korea

## Warning signs — seek urgent care

Seek urgent medical attention (in Korea, call 119; verify current emergency numbers) and inform your clinic if you notice:
- Blisters, open wounds or burns that are spreading or very painful
- Signs of infection such as increasing redness, warmth, pus or fever
- Signs of a severe allergic reaction such as facial or throat swelling
