## Overview

Ultherapy usually has minimal downtime. Lifting and tightening, when they appear, develop gradually over about 2–3 months. Always follow the specific instructions from your own clinic, as recommendations vary by individual.

## Procedure day

Mild redness, swelling and tenderness are common. Most people return to normal activities right away. Use gentle skincare and sunscreen.

## Days 1–3

Redness and swelling typically settle within hours to a few days. Tenderness when chewing or touching may continue.

## Days 4–7

Some tenderness may last for a few weeks. Avoid aggressive facial massage or treatments over the area.

## Weeks 2–4

Continue sun protection and hydration. Any welts or bruising along treatment lines usually fade.

## After 1 month

Results develop gradually and vary by individual. Ask your clinic about intervals before repeat treatment.

## Things to avoid

- Strong sun exposure without sunscreen
- Aggressive facial massage over the treated area for about 2 weeks
- Saunas and hot baths for a day or two

## When to contact the clinic

Contact your clinic if you notice:
- Numbness or tingling that lasts more than a few weeks
- Visible hollowing or welts that do not fade
- Swelling, bruising or pain that is getting worse after the first few days instead of better
- Redness, warmth or discharge at a treated or incision site
- Asymmetry or results you would like checked, especially before you leave Korea

## Warning signs — seek urgent care

Seek urgent medical attention (in Korea, call 119; verify current emergency numbers) and inform your clinic if you notice:
- Blisters, open wounds or burns that are spreading or very painful
- Signs of infection such as increasing redness, warmth, pus or fever
- Signs of a severe allergic reaction such as facial or throat swelling
