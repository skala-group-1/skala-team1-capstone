## Overview

This FAQ gives general information about paying for cosmetic procedures in Korea as a foreign visitor. Rules and clinic policies change over time, so always confirm the details with your clinic and official sources before you pay.

## VAT refund

**Q: Can foreigners get a VAT refund on cosmetic procedures in Korea?**
A: Korea has operated a tax refund scheme that allowed foreign tourists to reclaim VAT on certain cosmetic procedures (for example, some plastic surgery and dermatology treatments) at participating registered clinics. However, the scheme's eligibility, covered procedures, refund rates and even whether it is currently in effect have changed over time and may be extended, modified or ended. Always verify with the clinic and with official sources (such as the Korean tax authority or official tourism information) before assuming a refund is available.

**Q: How has the refund typically worked?**
A: Where available, it has generally involved a participating clinic issuing a refund document with your passport details, and claiming the refund at a designated counter or kiosk (often at the airport) or through a refund agent within a set period. Procedures, deadlines and minimum amounts may differ; ask the clinic for current steps.

**Q: Is my price quote VAT-inclusive or VAT-exclusive?**
A: It varies by clinic. Some quotes include VAT and some add it at payment. Ask for a written quote that clearly states whether VAT is included, and what the final amount would be with and without any expected refund.

## Payment methods

**Q: How can I pay?**
A: Many clinics accept major international credit and debit cards and cash in Korean won. Some may accept other methods such as bank transfer or certain mobile payments. Card payments may involve foreign transaction fees from your bank. Some clinics may offer different prices for cash versus card; ask for clarity and keep receipts in either case.

**Q: Do I need to pay a deposit?**
A: Clinics commonly ask for a deposit to secure a surgery date. Confirm the amount, how it is paid, and under what conditions it is refundable.

## Receipts and records

- Request an itemised receipt showing the procedure, date, amount, VAT and payment method.
- Keep copies of consent forms and medical records, which may be needed for follow-up care at home.
- Receipts may also be required for any tax refund process.

## Refunds and cancellations

**Q: What if I cancel or change my plans?**
A: Refund and cancellation policies depend on the clinic and on timing. Deposits may be partly or fully non-refundable close to the procedure date. Ask for the cancellation policy in writing before paying.

**Q: What about revisions or unsatisfactory results?**
A: Policies on revision surgery, touch-ups and associated costs vary widely. Clarify in advance what is covered, for how long, and whether you would need to travel back to Korea.

## Final reminder

Prices, tax rules and refund schemes change. Verify current information with your clinic and official government sources before making payment decisions.
