## Overview

Good preparation reduces stress and may help make your procedure and recovery safer. Use this checklist as a general guide; your clinic and doctor will give specific instructions for your procedure.

## Medical history

- [ ] List chronic conditions (for example, high blood pressure, diabetes, thyroid disease, bleeding disorders, autoimmune conditions).
- [ ] Note previous surgeries, cosmetic procedures, fillers, implants and any complications.
- [ ] Record allergies to medications, anaesthetics, latex or foods (some injectables may be derived from fish sources).
- [ ] Tell the clinic if you are pregnant, breastfeeding or planning pregnancy.
- [ ] For surgery, ask whether you need blood tests, ECG or other checks, and whether results from home are accepted.

## Medications and blood thinners

- [ ] Prepare a list of all medicines, including dosage, in English or Korean.
- [ ] Ask your own doctor before stopping any medication. Blood thinners (such as aspirin or anticoagulants) and some supplements (such as fish oil, vitamin E, ginkgo or garlic) may increase bleeding or bruising.
- [ ] Mention medications like isotretinoin, which may affect skin treatments.
- [ ] Stop smoking as early as possible before surgery; it may slow healing.

## Photos and goals

- [ ] Take clear photos of the treatment area from several angles in good lighting.
- [ ] Collect a few reference photos, but keep expectations realistic for your own anatomy.
- [ ] Write down your goals and questions in advance.

## Budget

- [ ] Request a written quote and ask whether it includes VAT, anaesthesia, medication, follow-ups, garments and revisions.
- [ ] Budget for accommodation, transport, meals and an emergency buffer.
- [ ] Check deposit and cancellation policies.

## Schedule buffer

- [ ] Plan extra days beyond the minimum recovery time in case of swelling or delays.
- [ ] Schedule follow-up visits and stitch removal before your departure date.
- [ ] Discuss flight timing with your surgeon before booking a return flight.

## Accommodation

- [ ] Choose a place near the clinic with easy transport access.
- [ ] Look for a quiet room, elevator access and nearby pharmacies and convenience stores.
- [ ] Consider recovery-friendly lodging with soft-food options for facial surgery.

## Companions

- [ ] Arrange a trusted companion for surgery days, especially if sedation or general anaesthesia is used.
- [ ] Share your itinerary and clinic contacts with family or friends at home.

## Documents

- [ ] Passport (needed for registration and tax refund processes).
- [ ] Travel and medical insurance details, noting cosmetic exclusions.
- [ ] Medication list, medical records and imaging.
- [ ] Clinic address and contact in Korean, plus after-hours number.
- [ ] Copies of consent forms, receipts and aftercare instructions to take home.
