## How does booking work?

All reservations require clinic approval. When you request a time, the slot is held for 30 minutes while the clinic reviews the request. The clinic may confirm it, or decline it and suggest alternative times. If no decision is made within 30 minutes, the hold is released.

## Can I cancel or change my reservation?

You can cancel from the app. Deposit and refund rules, if any, are set by each clinic; ask the clinic for its cancellation policy before confirming.
