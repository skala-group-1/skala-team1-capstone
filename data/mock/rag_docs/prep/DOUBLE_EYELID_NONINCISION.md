## Overview

Non-incision double eyelid surgery is a short eyelid procedure, often under local anaesthesia. Always follow the specific instructions from your own clinic, as recommendations vary by individual.

## One week before

- Stop smoking and review blood thinners and supplements with your doctor in advance.
- Plan your stay so that the follow-up check (and stitch removal if needed) happens before you fly home.

## Two days before

- Avoid alcohol from about two days before.
- Do not wear eyelash extensions; remove them in advance if needed.

## The day before

- Avoid alcohol.
- Follow any fasting instructions if sedation is planned.
- Prepare sunglasses and cold packs for after the procedure.

## On the day

- Come without eye makeup and without contact lenses; wear glasses instead.
- Wear a front-opening top so you do not pull clothing over your face.
- If sedation is used, arrange a companion and do not drive.

## What to bring

- Passport
- Medication list
- Sunglasses
- Glasses instead of contact lenses
- Clinic address and contact in Korean

## Tell the clinic in advance

- Dry eye, previous eye or eyelid surgery
- Blood thinners and bleeding problems
- Allergies to medicines or anaesthetics
