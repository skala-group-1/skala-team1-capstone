## Overview

Fat grafting is surgery with a donor area as well as the face. Always follow the specific instructions from your own clinic, as recommendations vary by individual.

## One week before

- Stop smoking as early as possible; it may slow healing.
- Review blood thinners, hormonal medicines and supplements with your doctor in advance.
- Confirm whether blood tests or other checks are needed and whether results from home are accepted.
- Arrange accommodation near the clinic and a companion for the surgery day.

## Two days before

- Avoid alcohol from at least two days before.
- Prepare soft foods, loose clothing and anything you need for the first days of recovery.

## The day before

- Follow the clinic's fasting instructions exactly (often no food or drink for several hours before anaesthesia).
- Avoid alcohol.
- Shower and remove nail polish, jewellery and piercings if asked.

## On the day

- Do not eat or drink if the clinic told you to fast.
- Wear loose, front-opening clothing and no makeup, lotion or jewellery.
- Bring your companion; you should not travel alone or drive after anaesthesia.

## What to bring

- Passport
- Medication list and medical records
- Test results the clinic asked for
- Loose, front-opening clothing
- Clinic address and contact in Korean

## Tell the clinic in advance

- All medical conditions, previous surgeries and complications
- Clotting disorders and all medicines
- Allergies, including to anaesthetics or latex
- Your return flight date
