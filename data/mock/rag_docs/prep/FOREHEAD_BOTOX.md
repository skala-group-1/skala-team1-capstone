## Overview

Forehead Botox is a short injection treatment with little downtime. Always follow the specific instructions from your own clinic, as recommendations vary by individual.

## One week before

- Ask your own doctor before stopping any medicine. Blood thinners and supplements such as fish oil, vitamin E or ginkgo may increase bruising.
- Avoid booking important photos or events in the first few days after treatment in case of small bruises.

## Two days before

- Avoid alcohol from about two days before, as it may increase bruising.
- Avoid new facial treatments in the area unless your clinic agrees.

## The day before

- Avoid alcohol and intense exercise.
- Get a good night's sleep and confirm your appointment time and the clinic address.

## On the day

- Come with a clean face, or plan to remove makeup at the clinic.
- Eat normally unless the clinic says otherwise.
- Allow about 30–60 minutes for consultation, numbing and treatment.

## What to bring

- Passport
- List of medicines and supplements
- Notes on previous toxin or filler treatments and dates

## Tell the clinic in advance

- Previous botulinum toxin treatment and when it was done
- Pregnancy, breastfeeding or neuromuscular conditions
- Allergies and current medicines
