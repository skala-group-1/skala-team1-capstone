## Overview

Nose filler is an injection treatment; avoid pressure on the nose afterwards. Always follow the specific instructions from your own clinic, as recommendations vary by individual.

## One week before

- Ask your own doctor before stopping any medicine. Blood thinners and supplements may increase bruising.
- If you get cold sores, tell the clinic early; preventive medication may be suggested.
- Allow at least one to two weeks before events or photos.

## Two days before

- Avoid alcohol from about two days before.
- Avoid facial treatments, peels or waxing near the area.

## The day before

- Avoid alcohol and intense exercise.
- Do not start new skincare products on the area.

## On the day

- Come without makeup on the treatment area if possible.
- Eat normally unless the clinic says otherwise.
- Plan for some swelling afterwards; avoid events the same evening.

## What to bring

- Passport
- List of medicines and supplements
- Reference photos, if you have them
- Records of previous fillers in the area

## Tell the clinic in advance

- Previous fillers, threads or implants in the area
- Cold sore history (for lips)
- Allergies, including to anaesthetic creams
- Pregnancy or breastfeeding
