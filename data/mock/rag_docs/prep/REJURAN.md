## Overview

Rejuran is a skin injection treatment that leaves small bumps for a few days. Always follow the specific instructions from your own clinic, as recommendations vary by individual.

## One week before

- Ask your own doctor before stopping any medicine. Blood thinners and supplements may increase bruising.
- Avoid strong sun and tanning.

## Two days before

- Pause retinoids, exfoliants and peels on the face unless your clinic says otherwise.
- Avoid alcohol from about two days before.

## The day before

- Avoid alcohol and saunas.
- Keep your skin calm; do not start new products.

## On the day

- Come with a clean face or plan to remove makeup at the clinic.
- Numbing cream may be applied for about 30 minutes before treatment.
- Expect small bumps afterwards; avoid events the same day.

## What to bring

- Passport
- List of medicines and supplements
- Sunscreen and a hat

## Tell the clinic in advance

- Fish allergy (some products are derived from fish sources)
- Recent skin treatments or skin conditions
- Pregnancy or breastfeeding
