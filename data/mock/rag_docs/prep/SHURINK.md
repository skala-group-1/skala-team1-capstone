## Overview

Shurink is a HIFU lifting treatment with minimal downtime. Always follow the specific instructions from your own clinic, as recommendations vary by individual.

## One week before

- Avoid tanning and sunburn on the area.
- Tell the clinic about photosensitising medicines and skin conditions.

## Two days before

- Pause retinoids, exfoliants and peels on the area unless your clinic says otherwise.
- Avoid waxing or plucking in the area.

## The day before

- Keep the skin calm and well moisturised.
- Avoid saunas and strong sun.

## On the day

- Come with clean skin and no makeup, lotion or deodorant on the area.
- Wear loose, comfortable clothing.
- Ask how long the session takes and whether numbing cream is used.

## What to bring

- Passport
- List of medicines
- Sunscreen and a hat

## Tell the clinic in advance

- Pacemakers, metal implants, fillers or threads in the area
- Recent tanning or sunburn
- Skin conditions, cold sores or keloid tendency
- Pregnancy
