## Overview

Jaw (masseter) Botox is a non-surgical injection treatment in which a small amount of botulinum toxin is placed into the masseter muscles at the angle of the jaw. It is commonly requested to soften a square or wide lower face, and it is sometimes used to ease clenching or teeth grinding (bruxism). It is one of the most frequently performed cosmetic injectables in Korea and is often combined with other skin treatments in the same visit.

**Also known as:** masseter Botox, jawline slimming injection, square jaw Botox, 사각턱 보톡스, 턱 보톡스, 瘦脸针, 咬肌肉毒, エラボトックス.

## How it works

Botulinum toxin temporarily blocks the signal between nerves and muscle fibres, which reduces how strongly the injected muscle contracts. When a bulky masseter is used less, it typically shrinks gradually over several weeks, which may produce a slimmer, more oval jaw contour. The effect is temporary because nerve signalling recovers over time. Results generally begin to show at around 2–4 weeks and are often most visible at 6–8 weeks, lasting roughly 3–6 months. Duration varies by individual, dose, muscle size and product; your doctor will advise.

## Procedure steps

1. **Consultation:** the doctor examines the jaw, asks you to clench to assess muscle bulk, and checks whether the width is mainly muscle, bone or fat.
2. **Preparation:** the skin is cleansed; numbing cream or ice may be offered, though many patients do not need it.
3. **Injection:** several small injections are given into each masseter at mapped points.
4. **Check-out:** you are usually able to leave immediately.

The injection itself typically takes about 5–10 minutes, and the whole visit often 20–40 minutes including consultation.

## Recovery & downtime

Downtime is usually minimal to none. Small red bumps or pinpoint bruises may appear at injection points and typically fade within hours to a few days. Many clinics advise not to massage or press the area, and to avoid lying face-down, saunas, intense exercise and alcohol for roughly the first day. Most people return to normal activities the same day.

## Common side effects

- Mild tenderness, redness or small bruises at injection sites
- Temporary chewing fatigue, especially with tough foods, for a few weeks
- Headache (uncommon)
- Uneven results or asymmetry, which may need a follow-up adjustment
- Less commonly, a change in smile shape if the product spreads to nearby muscles
- Rarely, hollowing below the cheekbone or a "sagging" impression with repeated high doses

Allergic reactions are rare. Discuss any history of neuromuscular conditions, pregnancy or breastfeeding with your doctor, as treatment is generally not recommended in these situations.

## Before your visit

- Tell the clinic about medications and supplements, especially blood thinners, which may increase bruising.
- Mention any previous botulinum toxin treatment and when it was done.
- Avoid scheduling important photos or events within the first few days in case of minor bruising.
- Note that visible slimming takes weeks, so you will usually see the final result after you return home.

## Questions to ask your doctor

- Is my jaw width mostly muscle, bone or fat, and is Botox suitable for me?
- Which product will be used, and how many units per side?
- When should I expect to see results, and how long will they likely last?
- Is a touch-up included if results are uneven?
- How often can this be safely repeated, and is there a risk of resistance over time?
