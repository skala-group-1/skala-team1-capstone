## Overview

Laser hair removal uses light energy to reduce unwanted hair on areas such as the underarms, legs, bikini line, face and arms. It aims for long-term hair reduction rather than permanent removal of every hair. Multiple sessions are needed, so it is usually best suited to people who live in or visit Korea regularly.

**Also known as:** laser epilation, permanent hair reduction, 레이저 제모, 겨드랑이 제모, 激光脱毛, 医疗脱毛, レーザー脱毛, 医療脱毛.

## How it works

Laser light (commonly diode, alexandrite or Nd:YAG wavelengths) is absorbed by the pigment in the hair. The heat travels to the follicle and damages its ability to regrow hair. Only follicles in the active growth phase respond well, which is why repeated sessions are needed. Typically 5–8 or more sessions spaced about 4–8 weeks apart are recommended, depending on the body area. Dark hair on lighter skin usually responds best; light, grey or red hair responds poorly. Results vary by individual; your doctor will advise.

## Procedure steps

1. **Consultation:** skin type and hair colour assessment, and a patch test may be done.
2. **Preparation:** the area is shaved (usually the day before or on site) and cleaned; numbing cream may be used for sensitive areas.
3. **Laser:** the handpiece is moved over the area with cooling; patients feel snapping or heat.
4. **Aftercare:** soothing gel or cooling is applied.

Small areas like the underarms take about 5–15 minutes; legs may take 30–60 minutes.

## Recovery & downtime

Downtime is usually minimal. Redness and small bumps around follicles typically settle within hours to a day or two. Treated hairs may shed over 1–3 weeks. Clinics commonly advise avoiding sun exposure, hot baths, saunas and intense exercise for a day or two, and not plucking or waxing between sessions (shaving is usually fine).

## Common side effects

- Temporary redness, swelling around follicles, and warmth
- Itching or mild discomfort
- Uncommonly, blisters, crusting or temporary pigment changes, especially in tanned or darker skin
- Rarely, burns or scarring
- Rarely, paradoxical increased hair growth in some areas

## Before your visit

- Avoid tanning and sunburn before treatment.
- Do not wax or pluck for several weeks before; shaving is usually recommended instead.
- Tell the clinic about photosensitising medications and skin conditions.
- If visiting briefly, understand that one session alone will typically give only partial reduction.

## Questions to ask your doctor

- Which laser type do you use, and is it suitable for my skin and hair colour?
- How many sessions should I expect for this area?
- Do you offer package pricing, and what happens if I cannot return for all sessions?
- How should I shave and prepare before each session?
- What should I do if I see blistering or dark spots afterwards?
