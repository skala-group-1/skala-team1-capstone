## Overview

Lip filler uses hyaluronic acid filler injected into the lips to add volume, define the lip border, improve symmetry or hydrate thin lips. In Korea, many patients ask for a natural look, such as a subtle "M-lip" shape or slightly fuller, rounder lips, rather than dramatic volume. Because hyaluronic acid filler is generally dissolvable, it is often chosen by people trying lip enhancement for the first time.

**Also known as:** lip augmentation, lip injections, 입술 필러, 립필러, 丰唇, 嘴唇玻尿酸, 唇ヒアルロン酸注入.

## How it works

Hyaluronic acid is a substance naturally present in the skin that binds water. Filler products use a stabilised (cross-linked) form that holds its shape in the tissue, adding volume and structure. Results are visible immediately, though early swelling makes the lips look larger at first. The final shape is usually clearer after about 1–2 weeks. Lip filler typically lasts around 6–12 months, as the lips move frequently and break down product faster than some other areas. Longevity varies by individual and product; your doctor will advise.

## Procedure steps

1. **Consultation:** discussion of the desired shape, a look at lip proportions, and agreement on the amount of filler (often 0.5–1 mL for a first session).
2. **Numbing:** a topical numbing cream is applied for about 20–30 minutes; many fillers also contain a local anaesthetic.
3. **Injection:** the doctor injects with a fine needle or blunt cannula, shaping as they go.
4. **Review:** you check the result in a mirror and the area is cleaned.

The injection typically takes about 15–30 minutes; the full visit often takes around an hour.

## Recovery & downtime

Swelling is common and usually peaks within 24–48 hours, settling mostly within 3–7 days. Bruising may last up to 1–2 weeks. Clinics commonly advise avoiding lipstick for a day, hot drinks and very spicy food for a short period, and saunas, intense exercise and alcohol for a few days. Many people return to work the next day, though the lips may look noticeably swollen at first.

## Common side effects

- Swelling, tenderness and bruising
- Small lumps or unevenness, which often settle or can be massaged or adjusted
- Cold-sore flare-ups in people with a history of herpes simplex
- Rarely, infection or delayed inflammatory reactions
- Rarely but importantly, filler blocking a blood vessel (vascular occlusion), which needs urgent treatment. Warning signs include severe pain, blanching or dusky, mottled skin.

Hyaluronic acid filler can usually be dissolved with an enzyme (hyaluronidase) if needed.

## Before your visit

- Tell the clinic if you get cold sores; preventive medication may be suggested.
- Report blood-thinning medications and supplements.
- Avoid booking just before events or photos; allow at least one to two weeks.
- Bring reference photos, but be prepared for the doctor to suggest what suits your face.

## Questions to ask your doctor

- Which filler product will you use, and is it hyaluronic acid?
- How much filler do you recommend for a natural result?
- Do you use a needle or cannula, and why?
- What should I do if I notice severe pain or skin colour change after leaving Korea?
- Is a follow-up or dissolving service included if I am unhappy?
