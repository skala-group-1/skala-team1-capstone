## Overview

Nose filler is a non-surgical way to reshape the nose by injecting hyaluronic acid filler. It is often used to raise a low nasal bridge, smooth a small bump, or add slight projection at the tip. It cannot make a nose smaller, narrow wide nostrils or correct breathing problems; those goals usually require surgery. It is popular with people who want to preview a change before considering rhinoplasty.

**Also known as:** non-surgical rhinoplasty, liquid nose job, 코 필러, 콧대 필러, 隆鼻玻尿酸, 注射隆鼻, 鼻ヒアルロン酸, プチ整形（鼻）.

## How it works

Hyaluronic acid filler is placed in small amounts along the bridge and sometimes the tip, creating height and a straighter profile. Results are visible immediately. Because the nose has limited soft tissue and an important blood supply, careful technique and conservative volume are especially important. Results typically last around 6–12 months, sometimes longer, depending on the product and individual; your doctor will advise.

## Procedure steps

1. **Consultation:** the doctor assesses your profile, skin thickness and any previous nose procedures.
2. **Numbing:** topical numbing cream or ice may be applied.
3. **Injection:** filler is placed with a fine needle or blunt cannula, usually at deep levels close to bone or cartilage.
4. **Shaping and review:** the doctor moulds the product and checks symmetry with you.

The procedure typically takes about 15–30 minutes.

## Recovery & downtime

Downtime is usually short. Mild swelling, redness or tenderness typically settles within a few days. Clinics commonly advise avoiding pressure on the nose (including heavy glasses) for about 1–2 weeks, not massaging unless instructed, and avoiding saunas, intense exercise and alcohol for a few days. Many people return to normal activities the same or next day.

## Common side effects

- Swelling, redness and minor bruising
- Slight asymmetry or a widened bridge if too much product is placed or it spreads
- Rarely, infection or delayed lumps
- Rarely but seriously, vascular occlusion — filler blocking a blood vessel — which can cause skin damage and, in very rare cases, vision problems. The nose is considered a higher-risk area for this. Severe pain, blanching, dusky skin or any change in vision needs immediate medical attention.

Hyaluronic acid filler can usually be dissolved with hyaluronidase if needed.

## Before your visit

- Tell the doctor about any prior nose surgery, implants or permanent fillers, as these can change the risks.
- Report blood thinners and supplements.
- Plan time to stay nearby for at least a day or two in case a check is needed.
- Keep your expectations realistic: filler adds height but does not reduce size.

## Questions to ask your doctor

- Am I a good candidate for filler, or would surgery suit my goals better?
- What product and how much will you use?
- What steps do you take to reduce the risk of vascular occlusion?
- What should I do if I notice warning signs after flying home?
- How long will the result last, and can it be dissolved?
