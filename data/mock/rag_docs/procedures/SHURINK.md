## Overview

Shurink is a Korean-developed lifting treatment that uses microfocused (high-intensity focused) ultrasound to tighten and lift the skin. It is commonly used to define the jawline, reduce mild sagging of the cheeks and under-chin, and improve contour. It is often positioned as a more affordable and less painful option compared with some other ultrasound devices, and is frequently repeated at shorter intervals.

**Also known as:** HIFU lifting, ultrasound lifting, 슈링크, 슈링크 유니버스, 슈링크 리프팅, 하이푸, 聚焦超声提拉, HIFU提升, シュリンク, ハイフ.

## How it works

Focused ultrasound energy is delivered to selected depths under the skin, creating small thermal points in deeper layers, including the supportive layer and fat. This is thought to contract tissue and stimulate collagen remodelling. Many patients notice slight tightening shortly after, with continued gradual improvement over 1–3 months. Results typically last several months, and some clinics suggest repeating every 3–6 months. Results vary by individual; your doctor will advise.

## Procedure steps

1. **Consultation:** assessment of skin laxity and agreement on areas and number of "shots" (lines).
2. **Preparation:** cleansing and, depending on the clinic, numbing cream.
3. **Treatment:** the handpiece is pressed against the skin and delivers rows of energy at chosen depths with different cartridges.
4. **Finish:** gel is cleaned off and soothing care may be applied.

Treatment typically takes about 20–40 minutes depending on shots and areas.

## Recovery & downtime

Downtime is usually minimal. Mild redness, swelling and a sensation of tightness or tenderness are common and usually settle within a day to a few days. Most people return to normal activities the same day. Gentle skincare, hydration and sun protection are recommended.

## Common side effects

- Discomfort, especially over bony areas
- Temporary redness, swelling or tenderness
- Uncommonly, bruising or welts along treatment lines
- Rarely, temporary numbness or nerve irritation
- Rarely, unwanted fat loss or hollowing, especially in thin faces
- Rarely, burns with inappropriate settings

## Before your visit

- Note that "shot counts" vary by clinic and are not always comparable; ask what is included.
- Mention recent fillers, threads or other energy treatments.
- Tell the clinic about skin conditions or infections in the treatment area.
- Consider combining with other treatments only if the doctor confirms it is appropriate.

## Questions to ask your doctor

- How many shots do you recommend for my face, and at which depths?
- Is this suitable for my face shape, or could it make my cheeks look hollow?
- How does this compare with other ultrasound or radiofrequency options for me?
- How often should I repeat it?
- Can it be done on the same day as Botox or filler?
