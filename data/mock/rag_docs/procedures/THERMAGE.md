## Overview

Thermage is a non-invasive skin tightening treatment that uses monopolar radiofrequency energy to heat the deeper layers of skin. It is commonly used on the face, around the eyes, the neck and sometimes the body to improve mild laxity, crepey texture and fine lines. It is often compared with ultrasound lifting; the two are sometimes combined because they work at different depths.

**Also known as:** RF skin tightening, radiofrequency lift, 써마지, 써마지 FLX, 热玛吉, サーマクール.

## How it works

Monopolar radiofrequency delivers controlled heat volumetrically into the dermis and the tissue below it, while the skin surface is cooled to protect it. Heat is thought to cause existing collagen fibres to contract and to stimulate new collagen production over time. Some tightening may be felt immediately, but improvement usually develops gradually over 2–6 months and may last around 1–2 years. Results vary by individual; your doctor will advise.

## Procedure steps

1. **Consultation:** assessment of skin laxity and discussion of target areas and number of "shots".
2. **Preparation:** cleansing, optional numbing cream, and placement of a temporary grid on the skin to guide treatment.
3. **Treatment:** the handpiece is applied across each area in passes, with cooling and heating cycles. Patients usually feel brief heat sensations.
4. **Finish:** the grid is removed and the skin is soothed.

A face treatment typically takes about 45–90 minutes depending on the number of shots.

## Recovery & downtime

Downtime is usually minimal. Mild redness and warmth typically fade within hours; slight swelling may last a day or two. Most people return to normal activities, including makeup, the same or next day. Sun protection and gentle skincare are recommended.

## Common side effects

- Heat discomfort during treatment
- Temporary redness, swelling or tenderness
- Rarely, small blisters, burns or temporary surface irregularities
- Rarely, temporary numbness
- Very rarely, fat loss leading to a subtle hollowed appearance

## Before your visit

- Tell the clinic about pacemakers or implanted electronic devices; monopolar radiofrequency is generally not suitable in this case.
- Mention metal implants, fillers, thread lifts or recent procedures in the area.
- Ask how many shots are included and confirm genuine tips are used.
- Allow a margin before events in case of temporary redness.

## Questions to ask your doctor

- Is Thermage or ultrasound lifting more suitable for my concerns, or both?
- How many shots do you recommend for my face, and is the eye area included?
- How do you confirm genuine consumables are used?
- What does it feel like, and what pain relief can you offer?
- When should I expect results, and how often can it be repeated?
