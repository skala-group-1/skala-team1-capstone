## Overview

Ultherapy is a non-surgical skin lifting and tightening treatment using microfocused ultrasound. It is commonly used for mild to moderate sagging of the brow, cheeks, jawline, under-chin and neck. It is not a replacement for a surgical facelift, but may suit people who want gradual, subtle lifting without incisions.

**Also known as:** ultrasound lift, HIFU lift (high-intensity focused ultrasound), non-surgical lift, 울쎄라, 超声刀, 极线超声刀, ウルセラ.

## How it works

Microfocused ultrasound energy is delivered through the skin to specific depths, including the deeper supportive layer (often called the SMAS) that surgeons tighten in a facelift. The energy creates tiny heated points that are thought to cause immediate tissue contraction and stimulate new collagen over time. Many devices use real-time ultrasound imaging to help target the right depth. A slight effect may be seen immediately, but results usually build over 2–3 months and may last around 1 year or more. Results vary by individual; your doctor will advise.

## Procedure steps

1. **Consultation:** assessment of skin laxity, skin thickness and suitability.
2. **Preparation:** cleansing, marking of treatment zones, and numbing cream or oral pain relief depending on the clinic.
3. **Treatment:** a handpiece is moved across the face and neck, delivering lines of energy at set depths. The number of "shots" or lines affects price and result.
4. **Finish:** gel is removed; no dressing is needed.

A full face and neck treatment typically takes about 45–90 minutes.

## Recovery & downtime

Downtime is usually minimal. Mild redness, swelling and tenderness are common and typically settle within hours to a few days. Some people feel tenderness when chewing or touching the area for a few weeks. Most return to normal activities right away. Gentle skincare and sun protection are recommended.

## Common side effects

- Discomfort or pain during treatment, which varies by area and settings
- Temporary redness, swelling, tingling or tenderness
- Bruising or welts along treatment lines (uncommon)
- Rarely, temporary numbness or nerve irritation
- Rarely, fat loss causing hollowing if energy is placed in thin areas
- Rarely, burns if settings or technique are unsuitable

## Before your visit

- Mention any facial implants, fillers, thread lifts, metal plates or recent procedures.
- Disclose skin conditions, active acne or open wounds in the treatment area.
- Ask about pain control in advance, as comfort levels vary.
- Confirm that the device is genuine and ask how many lines/shots are included in the quoted price.

## Questions to ask your doctor

- Am I a good candidate, or is my sagging too advanced for ultrasound treatment?
- How many lines and which depths will you use for my face?
- How is genuine device use verified (for example, cartridge confirmation)?
- What pain relief options are available?
- How soon will I see results, and when would a repeat treatment make sense?
